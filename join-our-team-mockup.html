Why Resource Room

A place educators actually want to stay.

We built Resource Room because we were tired of watching great teachers burn out in systems that didn't let them teach. Here's what's different.

01

Small by Design

Low student-to-teacher ratios mean you actually get to teach, not just manage a room.

02

Family-Run

Owned and operated by licensed educators, not a franchise or private equity roll-up. Decisions get made by people who've been in your seat.

03

Flexible Schedules

Part-time, full-time, seasonal camp work, we build roles around real life, not the other way around.

04

Room to Grow

Start as a tutor, move into camp leadership, coaching, or admissions. We promote from within, often.

Open Roles
