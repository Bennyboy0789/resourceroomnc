Rolls into Summer Camp
No weeks to show. Choose another track.
Dates follow the Wake County Public School System 2026-27 multi-track year-round calendar. Camp runs on most district holidays, including Veterans Day, MLK Day, Presidents’ Day, and Election Day. We are closed Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after, December 24 and 25, December 31, January 1, and Memorial Day. Booking is simple: choose Single Day or Full Week at checkout, then enter the exact dates you need in the order notes and we will confirm by email. Rescheduling a camp week is subject to a 30 percent fee, and there is no refund within 2 weeks of the start date, since materials are purchased per camper per week. If the district adjusts the calendar for weather makeup days, we will update this page and email registered families.
Bonus camp days · every track out at once

What the day looks like
A school day made of only the good parts
Same rhythm every day, posted on the wall where kids can see it. Enough structure that nobody is guessing what comes next, enough variety that nobody gets bored.
8:00 – 8:30
Arrival and soft start
Quiet tables, puzzles, drawing, and a chance to settle in before the day gets going.
8:30 – 10:00
Hands on science
Slime, volcanoes, elephant toothpaste, oobleck, and experiments that spark real curiosity.
10:00 – 11:30
Building and engineering
LEGO challenges, robotics, design thinking, and problem solving that takes a whole team to crack.
11:30 – 1:00
Lunch and outdoor time
Bagged lunch, then nature walks, outdoor games, and fresh air before the afternoon.
1:00 – 2:30
Coding and digital challenges
Interactive coding games and puzzles that build logic and sequencing without feeling like a lesson.
2:30 – 3:30
Crafts, creativity, and wrap up
Projects that let kids create and build their own ideas. Clean up, share the day, and get ready for pickup.
