STEAM Programs at Resource Room NC
Resource Room provides a comprehensive STEAM program offering that scaffold accordingly to build on student knowledge and engagement. We offer a variety of Curriculum Bases STEAM classes focused on promoting learning, team building, and an in-depth exploration of various academic disciplines.
Request A Consultation
Discover the Wonders of STEAM at Resource Room NC! &#x1f680;
At Resource Room, we bring the exciting world of STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics) to life for young minds! Our comprehensive program is specially designed to build on your child’s curiosity and knowledge, step by step. Join us for an adventure in learning, teamwork, and deep exploration across various academic disciplines.
Developed by licensed teachers, our curriculum promises a fun and engaging educational journey. It’s structured to inspire and foster a love for learning in every child.
What Exciting STEAM Programs Do We Offer?
Drones: The Sky’s the Limit! &#x1f681;
Grades K-8 will marvel at the science of flight, from basic principles to advanced drone technology. Watch your child’s imagination take flight as they learn about automated flight, drone delivery, and more!
Robotics: Building the Future! &#x1f916;
Ideal for Grades K-8, our Robotics program introduces the basics of coding and automation. Your child will learn how robots work and even try their hand at programming!
3-D Printing: Bringing Ideas to Life! &#x1f5a8;️
Available for Grades 3-8, this program explores the fascinating world of 3-D printing. Watch as your child’s creations come to life, learning about programming, design, and much more.
Lego Robotics and Exploration: Construct, Create, Collaborate! &#x1f9f1;
Our Lego Education Courses are all about hands-on learning and teamwork. It’s a perfect blend of science, engineering, and creativity for all ages.
Why Choose Resource Room for STEAM Learning?
Resource Room is more than just a learning center – it’s a hub of “funducational” experiences! We blend interactive, foundational, and advanced concepts in Science and Math to make learning accessible and exciting. Our STEAM programs emphasize skills like teamwork, communication, and community planning – essential for both academic and professional growth. STEAM education at Resource Room is:
Inclusive for all types of learners
A great way to promote socialization
Rich with real-world applications and concepts
Hands-On Learning:
Hands-on Science Experiments: Get ready for fun and discovery with activities like fluffy slime, volcanic eruptions, and elephant toothpaste!
Creative LEGO Building Challenges: Imagination meets engineering as campers build everything from bridges to roller coasters.
Artistic Crafts and Self-Expression: Campers will craft, design, and express themselves in countless creative ways.
Outdoor Adventures and Nature Exploration: We believe in the power of outdoor learning – from nature walks to outdoor games, there’s always something new to explore.
Coding Challenges and Digital Literacy: Dive into the digital world with coding adventures, from programming LEGO® robots to creating animated stories.
Join us at Resource Room NC for a STEAM experience your child will never forget – where learning, fun, and imagination come together to create something extraordinary! &#x1f31f;
Resource Room was established in 2015 by career educators. The husband and wife team of Sam and Joe recognized the need for a comprehensive learning center, where students can find academic support and programs from Pre-K through the end of their high school career. We are supported by a professional team and a dedicated client base. A family owned business that aims to service the members of their community with a professional and personalized approach.
