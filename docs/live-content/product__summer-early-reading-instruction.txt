Early Reading Instruction
Join us for private, individualized early childhood literacy instruction with a focus on phonics.
Request A Consultation
Welcome to the Resource Room Learning Center’s Early Reading Instruction Program
Introduction to Reading for Kindergarten and First Grade Students
We invite you to enroll your child in our specialized early childhood literacy program, focused on the foundational elements of phonics. At the Resource Room Learning Center, we are committed to providing individualized and private instruction tailored to the unique learning style of each young reader.
Enhance Your Child’s Literacy Skills with Our Phonics-Based Approach
Our program is designed to guide emerging readers in developing phonetic awareness, a critical skill for early literacy. We focus on the mastery of vowel and consonant sounds, as well as the blending of these sounds, to form the building blocks of reading.
A Comprehensive 10-Week Early Reading Program
Prepare your child to excel in reading with our 10-week program, meticulously crafted for young learners. This program is aimed at instilling confidence and proficiency in reading from an early age.
Contact Us for Further Information and Free Consultation
We welcome your inquiries and interest in our program. Please feel free to call, text, or contact us through our website. Our team is available to answer any questions and to schedule a complimentary consultation to discuss your child’s reading journey.
Exploring the Components of Early Reading Instruction
Phonics Instruction
Phonics is an integral method in teaching young children the relationship between letters and sounds, forming the foundation for word recognition and decoding skills necessary for reading fluency and comprehension.
Word Recognition Enhancement
Our instruction in phonics will improve your child’s ability to recognize and identify words, enhancing their reading skills and allowing them to access a broader range of texts.
Development of Spelling and Writing Abilities
The program also focuses on spelling and writing, enabling children to understand sound-letter correspondences and apply this knowledge in their spelling, leading to accurate and independent writing.
Vocabulary Expansion
A significant aspect of our program is aiding children in the development of their vocabulary. As they learn phonetic patterns, they can decode unfamiliar words, thereby enriching their language skills.
Advancement in Reading Comprehension
Phonics instruction is a vital step towards achieving reading comprehension. It facilitates a better understanding of text by enabling children to focus more on the meaning rather than struggling with word decoding.
Promotion of Independent Reading
Our early phonics instruction empowers children to become autonomous readers, confident in exploring texts independently, fostering a lifelong love for reading and learning.
At the Resource Room Learning Center, we are dedicated to laying a strong foundation for successful reading and language development in young children. Through our program, we equip them with the essential skills to become proficient and confident readers.
