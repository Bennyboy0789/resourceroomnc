Pathways Academy
Pathways at Resource Room is a newly approved private high school located in Holly Springs, NC, created for neurodiverse learners with autism in grades 9–12. Our program is designed to meet the needs of students who thrive with personalized support, structure, and hands-on learning — offering a fresh start for those who may not have found success in traditional school settings. At Pathways, we help students build confidence, independence, and real-world readiness in a supportive, understanding environment.

The Problem Traditional Schools Can’t Solve
Many neurodiverse teens are bright, capable, and full of potential — yet struggle in traditional school settings. The challenge is rarely the student. It’s the environment.
Traditional classrooms were not designed for how many neurodiverse learners process information, manage tasks, and engage with learning. IEP accommodations can offer helpful support, but they cannot fundamentally reshape the learning environment itself.
Pathways Academy was created to do exactly that.
Instead of asking students to adapt to a system that wasn’t built for them, we built a school around how neurodiverse teens actually learn — with structure, individualized pacing, daily executive functioning support, and educators who understand the difference.
What Makes Pathways Different
Pathways Academy operates as a private high school with a social, executive function, and project-based learning model designed specifically for neurodiverse learners. Students work toward earning a private high school diploma through a path that is intentionally flexible and individualized.
At Pathways, academics are one part of a larger equation. Success after high school requires organization, persistence, communication, problem-solving, and confidence navigating the real world. Our program develops the whole student — not just coursework completion.
Personalized Academic Pathways
Coursework scaled to each student’s readiness and ability. Small classes and individualized pacing allow students to build academic momentum with the support they need to succeed.
Executive Function Development
Organization, task initiation, planning, and follow-through — explicitly taught and practiced daily through structured routines and guided support.
Social Development and Communication
Collaboration, conversation skills, and self-advocacy strengthened through daily structured interaction in a safe, supportive environment.
Project-Based and Hands-On Learning
Meaningful projects that connect academic skills to real situations — developing creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving alongside genuine confidence.
Career Exploration and Real-World Experiences
Internships, trade exposure, and community-based experiences that help students explore interests and develop a vision for their future.
Is Pathways Academy the Right Fit?
Pathways may be a strong fit for students who:
Are neurodiverse learners, including students with autism, ADHD, anxiety, or learning differences
Feel overwhelmed or disengaged in traditional school settings
Benefit from smaller learning environments and individualized pacing
Need structured routines and predictable daily schedules
Need support with organization, task initiation, and executive functioning
Learn best through hands-on, project-based, and real-world experiences
May be academically behind their current grade level and need a path to catch up
Many of our students are bright and capable but have found that traditional school settings did not match how they learn. Pathways provides a structured environment where students rebuild confidence, develop life skills, and make steady progress toward earning their diploma.
Our mission: To help students build readiness for life, learning, and greater independence in an environment designed for how neurodiverse teens learn best.
Tuition & Funding — Making Pathways Accessible
Pathways Academy’s full tuition is $24,000 per year — but most families pay significantly less. North Carolina offers two grant programs that can cover most or all of tuition. We help every family navigate both.
$17,000
ESA+ Grant (max — autism diagnosis)
North Carolina’s ESA+ program provides funding for students with qualifying disabilities. Students with an autism diagnosis may receive up to $17,000 annually toward private school tuition.
$7,000+
Opportunity Scholarship (income-tiered)
Available to any family pursuing school choice. Unlike ESA+, there is no waitlist. Combined with ESA+, many families can cover the full cost of tuition.
&#x1f4a1; We help you access every dollar available. Many families don’t know these programs exist or don’t know how to apply. We walk you through both, help with paperwork, and work with your family to make tuition work. A consultation is always free.
