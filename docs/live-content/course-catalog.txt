Trackout & Summer Camps
Looking for an educational experience during Track Out or for your home-schooled student? Our STEM and Enrichment Programs offer a unique opportunity for students to learn in a hands-on “funducational” manner during the day.
Learn More
Minecraft Education
Minecraft
has a plethora of features where students can experiment with levers, switches, and electrical circuits aka redstone. Students will be able to create farming systems or harness energy to create water cycles and renewable energy. Minecraft
also enables a space for students to visualize and investigate different situations where they can create solutions which helps them to develop critical and spatial thinking.
Learn More
Roblox Gaming Education
Learn game design through Roblox. Roblox has a creative platform that can teach the basics of game design. During our programs we learn how to make different shapes and characters. Children will build learning experiences designed for exploration, investigation, and experimentation. At the end of the course your child will be able to build a full obstacle course!
Learn More
Lego BriQ
This program offers students the opportunity to learn about and explore kinetic forces through hands-on experiences. Students will build out LEGO designs that will allow them to learn about forces such as gravity, friction, propulsion & more through play. Each class will engage students with hands-on activities as well as guided lessons to develop their understanding of the physical world.
Learn More
Robotics
Students will have the opportunity to learn how to code in the mBlockly application and program their own mBot robots. The final lessons of the course include a team competition in which students will apply the skills they’ve learned and a debate in which students consider important ethical questions around the use of robots in the modern world.
Intro To Robots (Grades K-2)
Discovery of Robots (Grades 3-5)
Exploration of Robots (Grades 6-8)
Drones
Students will be introduced to drones. Students will experience how flight occurs and what is necessary for animals and objects to take flight. Students learn how to program drone flight through coding in the Droneblocks application.
Intro To Drones (Grades K - 2)
Discovery of Drones (Grades 3-5)
Exploration of Drones (Grades 6-8)
SAT Test Prep
Professionally administered SAT instruction from licensed, career educators. Course includes in-person instruction, proctored diagnostic assessments, and homework and practice assignments.

We are supported by a professional team and a dedicated client base. A family owned business that aims to service the members of their community with a professional and personalized approach.
