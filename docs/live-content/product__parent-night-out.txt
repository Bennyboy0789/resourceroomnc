Parents Night Out in Holly Springs
STEM Fun for Kids, A Night Off for You
Looking for a safe, structured, and genuinely fun night for your child while you enjoy a well-earned evening out?
At Resource Room Learning Center, our Parents Night Out program in Holly Springs is more than babysitting—it’s an engaging STEM experience designed to keep kids active, social, and learning.
What Makes Our Parent Night Different
This is not just free play.
Every Parents Night Out is built around:
Hands-on STEM activities
Creative building challenges
Structured group games
High-energy, supervised fun
Kids are engaged the entire time
Parents get peace of mind
Themes: Fizzy, Bubbly Fun
Your child will dive into a night of:
LEGO building challenges
Hands-on science experiments (fizz, bubbles, safe “explosions”)
Team-based games and problem-solving activities
Pizza, snacks, and water included
It’s learning disguised as fun—and kids love it
Why Parents Choose Resource Room
We are not a drop-off play center.
We are educators.
That means:
Activities are intentional and structured
Staff are experienced educators
Students are engaged, supported, and supervised
Your child is not just watched—they are actively learning and participating
Who This Is Perfect For
Kids who enjoy building, experimenting, and creating
Students who need structured, engaging environments
Children who thrive with hands-on learning
Kids who want to have fun while making friends
Parents Night Out Near You (Holly Springs, NC)
Resource Room provides a trusted, local option for parents looking for kids night out programs, STEM activities, and safe evening childcare alternatives.
